Storage

Disk controller
Linux packages
Debian's packages for this processor, fetched once (about 1.9 GB for Arm, 1.3 GB for x86-64) into target/linux-root (-aarch64 for Arm) and put on the disk as /Linux. Off: a smaller disk, NetSurf as the browser.
Developer disk
Boots target/waveos-developer.img instead of your everyday disk, with room for WaveSDK work.
Fresh disk
This erases files saved inside WaveOS (notes, documents, settings). You'll be asked to confirm. It's never remembered for Quick launch.

Erase the WaveOS disk?

A fresh disk replaces target/waveos-aurora.img. Everything saved inside WaveOS on it (notes, documents, folders, settings) is erased. This can't be undone.

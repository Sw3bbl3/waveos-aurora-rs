# Settings
Personalize the desktop and configure the system.

## Use the app
1. Open Settings and select Appearance, Desktop & Dock, or Accessibility.
2. Adjust theme, accent, wallpaper, glass intensity, dock size, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, or Increase Contrast.
3. Press Ctrl+F to search for a specific control. Use Tab/Shift+Tab to move between search, navigation, and controls.
4. In Display, choose a mode and confirm Keep only if the desktop remains usable. Restart to verify saved preferences.

## Expected result and troubleshooting
Other pages cover keyboard layouts/repeat, date/time, sound, notifications, networking, and system information. QEMU VGA supports live mode changes; other displays may require restarting. Read-only or full storage can prevent saving preferences.

See [Desktop and windows](desktop-windows.md) for shared shortcuts and [Recovery](reference-recovery.md) if the app cannot open.

## Search directly
Search by a label or keyword, such as “motion,” “volume,” or “keyboard repeat.” Results show the control and its page. Use Up/Down and Enter, or click a result, to reveal and focus it without changing its value. Escape clears the query. Existing page launch arguments still work; a destination such as `Settings.elf accessibility/motion` opens a specific control.

# Search, notifications, and Control Center
Find apps and files without navigating folders.

## Use Spotlight
1. Press Super+Space or Ctrl+Space.
2. Type an app, file, Settings page, calculation such as `12*7+1`, or command such as `dark mode`.
3. Select a result with the arrow keys and press Enter. Press Escape to close.

Spotlight searches apps and indexed file names; Learn provides full-text guide search.

## Notifications and quick controls
1. Click the clock to open Notification Center and its calendar.
2. Use Do Not Disturb to suppress banners.
3. Click the sliders icon in the menu bar to open Control Center.
4. Change appearance, Do Not Disturb, or volume, or open Settings. Tab moves focus, Space/Enter activates buttons and toggles, and arrows adjust sliders.

## Troubleshooting
Click outside a panel or press Escape to dismiss it. Missing network or battery controls may indicate that the corresponding hardware was not detected. Changing Do Not Disturb does not mute audio; use volume separately.

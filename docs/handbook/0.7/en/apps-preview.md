# Preview
View PNG, JPEG, GIF, and BMP images.

## Use the app
1. Open Pictures in Files.
2. Open an image to launch Preview.
3. Use the available zoom controls to inspect the picture.

## Expected result and troubleshooting
Unsupported or damaged images may fail to decode. Preview is an image viewer; use Paint to draw. SVG and WebP are not supported.

See [Desktop and windows](desktop-windows.md) for shared shortcuts and [Recovery](reference-recovery.md) if the app cannot open.

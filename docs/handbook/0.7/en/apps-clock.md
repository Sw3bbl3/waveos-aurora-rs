# Clock
Use world clocks, alarms, a stopwatch, and timers.

## Use the app
1. Open Clock and choose the required mode.
2. Set the time or duration using the displayed controls.
3. Start a short timer and verify its completion notification and sound.

## Expected result and troubleshooting
Check Settings → Date & Time if clocks are wrong. Check volume and Do Not Disturb if you do not hear or see an alert. Do not rely on an untested powered-off alarm workflow.

See [Desktop and windows](desktop-windows.md) for shared shortcuts and [Recovery](reference-recovery.md) if the app cannot open.

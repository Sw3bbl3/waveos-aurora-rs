Welcome to WaveOS Aurora 0.7.0

Open Learn for the complete offline handbook in English or French (Canada).
Use Ctrl+Space to find an app, or open Launcher from the dock.
Hold Alt and press Tab to choose a window. Super+arrow keys arrange windows.

Files and preferences survive restarts when a writable AuroraFS home volume
is mounted. Use the system menu to shut down cleanly. An ISO session without
a writable home volume stores its files temporarily in memory.

Try creating a folder in Files, saving a note in Notes, or changing the
appearance in Settings. Constellation Studio can edit projects; its native
compiler and debugger remain unavailable in this release.

Welcome to WaveOS Aurora!

Everything you see was built from scratch: the bootloader, the Aster kernel,
the Lumen Server window server, the storage drivers, the AuroraFS filesystem and every
app. Each app runs as its own protected user-space process.

Your files are stored on disk in AuroraFS, a journaled filesystem, so they
survive restarts and even power cuts.

Try:
  • Opening apps from the launcher (or press the Windows/Super key)
  • Creating a folder in Files, or editing this note and saving it
  • Typing `ps`, `mem` or `ls -l | grep txt` in Terminal
  • Switching to dark mode in Settings (it is remembered)
